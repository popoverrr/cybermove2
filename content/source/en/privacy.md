# Privacy policy
## What data we collect
Through the website form: name, contact (phone, WhatsApp or Telegram), company name or website, the chosen engagement format and the message. Technically: interface language, the source page and the UTM parameters of the visit.
## Why
To answer the request and propose an engagement format. Data is not shared with third parties except the delivery services configured for the site (e-mail, Telegram, CRM).
