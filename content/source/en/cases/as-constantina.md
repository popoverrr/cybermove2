## Project
AS CONSTANTINA is a container ship built in 2005: 221.69 m long, 29.86 m beam, 37,883 t deadweight, Liberia flag. One of the vessels the CYBERMOVE team worked with in ship management.
## CYBERMOVE role
Management of shipboard organisation: crew and watches, onboard processes and procedures, supplies and bunkering, documentation, reporting and liaison with the operator, ports and the classification society.
## Result
Vessel in regular service: voyages and port calls on schedule, no downtime for organisational reasons.
