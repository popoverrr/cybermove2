# Business phone system and call tracking setup
We connect and configure telephony so every call is recorded and linked to a deal in the CRM and to the ad channel the customer came from. For companies where sales and service start with a phone call.
SITE · CRM · VOIP · AI · AUTO
When calls go to managers' personal numbers, the company does not know how many customers called, how many could not get through or what they were told. A cloud VoIP system fixes that: one shared number, call routing, recording and statistics. Integration with the CRM and call tracking turns each call into data: where the customer came from, who answered, how the conversation ended and what the lead cost.
01
## What's included
### Setup and configuration
Numbers, lines and extensions, working from a phone, a computer or on the road.
### Call routing
Queues, forwarding, working hours and an IVR menu only where it really helps.
### Call recording
All calls are recorded and available to management for quality control and training.
### Missed calls
Every missed call becomes a call-back task instead of disappearing.
### Call tracking
Dynamic numbers per ad channel show which campaign drives calls and what each call costs.
### CRM integration
A call creates or finds a deal, and the recording is attached to the customer record.
### Reports
Call volume, missed calls, answer time and workload per employee.

## FAQ
Do we need to change our number? |
Not necessarily. An existing number can often be ported or forwarded to the new system, with tracking numbers added for advertising.
How much does it cost? |
It combines the provider's plan — based on lines and minutes — with the scope of our setup. We quote after the free initial review.
Is call recording legal? |
Recording business calls is common practice, but customers are usually informed at the start of the call. We set up that notice and store recordings with restricted access.
Does it work on mobile phones? |
Yes, staff can make and take calls on the shared number through an app on their phone or computer.
What is call tracking and do we need it? |
It swaps the phone number on your site depending on the visitor's source, so you can see which ads drive calls. You need it if a meaningful share of customers call rather than fill in forms.
Can telephony connect to our CRM? |
In most cases yes: popular CRMs and VoIP providers have ready integrations. If not, we connect them via API or an integration platform.
