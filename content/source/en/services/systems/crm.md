# CRM implementation services
We set up a CRM around the way your company actually sells: pipelines, automated tasks, and integrations with your website, ads, phones and messengers. The goal is that no lead gets lost and management sees sales without manual reports.
SITE · CRM · VOIP · AI · AUTO
A CRM installed out of the box quickly turns into an expensive address book: managers run deals in messengers, data drifts apart and nobody trusts the reports. So we start by describing your sales process, not by picking buttons. We configure the system so it is easier for a manager to work in it than around it, and the owner gets pipeline, conversion and source figures without spreadsheets assembled by hand.

## What's included
### Choosing a platform
Comparing options for your process, budget and required integrations; data migration if you already have a CRM.

Deal stages, required fields and loss reasons — exactly as much as you need to see the picture.
### Automated tasks and scenarios
Reminders, follow-ups, lead distribution between managers, reactions to missed calls.

Website, forms, ad accounts, telephony, WhatsApp and other messengers, payment services.

Pipeline, stage conversion, lead sources, response time and each manager's performance.
### Team training
Guides and walkthroughs on real deals, so the CRM gets used rather than tolerated.
### Ongoing support
Adjustments after the first weeks, new scenarios and discipline checks.

## FAQ
Which CRM should we choose — Bitrix24, amoCRM or something else? |
It depends on your sales process, the number of managers, required integrations and budget. We compare options against your process and explain the pros and limits of each rather than selling one system.
How much does CRM implementation cost? |
It combines the platform's licence fees with the scope of configuration: pipelines, integrations, scenarios. We quote our part after the free initial review.
How long does implementation take? |
Usually two to six weeks to go-live, followed by refinement based on the first weeks of data. Timing depends on integrations and data migration.
Can you migrate data from our old system or spreadsheets? |
Yes. We move contacts, deals and history if an export is available. Before migrating we remove duplicates and agree on what should not be moved.
What if managers refuse to use the CRM? |
That is the main reason implementations fail. We configure the system around their work, remove unnecessary fields, train on live deals and give management a discipline report.
