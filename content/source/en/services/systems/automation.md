# Business process automation
We remove manual work between your tools: leads, invoices, reports and notifications flow automatically, and management sees the business on one screen. For companies whose processes live in employees' memory and spreadsheets.
SITE · CRM · VOIP · AI · AUTO
Digitalisation often means buying tools that know nothing about each other. The website, CRM, accounting, ads and messengers live separately, and people copy data by hand and make mistakes. We start with a process map — where the routine is, where errors happen, where time is lost — and automate what brings the biggest effect, connecting tools through Make, n8n or direct APIs.

### Process map
How leads, orders, documents and reports really flow; where manual work sits and where time is lost.

Leads from every channel into the CRM, notifications, follow-ups and data sent back to ad platforms.

From ad click to payment: which channel brings revenue, not just leads.

Templated documents, reminders, reconciliations, exports and notifications that are done by hand today and cause errors.

Key business metrics on one screen, updated without anyone's involvement.

## FAQ
Which processes should be automated first? |
The ones that repeat most often and where mistakes cost most: lead hand-off, invoicing, reporting. We start there so the effect is visible immediately.
How do Make and n8n differ from custom development? |
They are integration platforms: scenarios are built faster and cheaper than code from scratch and are easier to change. For non-standard tasks we use direct APIs.
How much does automation cost? |
It depends on the number of processes and tools to connect. After the process map we price each scenario; the initial review is free.
What if a tool changes or breaks? |
Scenarios are documented and failure alerts are set up. Under a support plan we track tool changes and fix integrations before customers notice.
Do we have to replace our tools? |
Usually not. We first connect what you already use and suggest replacing a tool only where it blocks automation.
How much time will automation save? |
We do not promise a number upfront: it depends on the volume of routine. During mapping we measure how long each operation takes now and compare after launch.
