# AI agents for business
We put AI where it saves your team time: qualifying leads, answering routine questions, analysing calls and chats, preparing management reports. AI that works inside the process rather than impressing in a presentation.
SITE · CRM · VOIP · AI · AUTO
Bringing AI into a business starts with a question, not a model: which routine will it take over, and how will we check the result? A good AI agent answers customers at any hour, asks clarifying questions and hands a prepared lead to a manager; a bad one confidently states things that are not true. So we limit the agent to your data, spell out what it must not do and keep a human in charge wherever mistakes are expensive.

## What's included
### Lead qualification
The agent clarifies the task, budget and timing, scores the lead and passes a prepared customer to a manager.
### AI chatbot for sales
Answers to frequent questions on the website and in messengers, booking consultations, handing complex questions to a person.
### Call and chat analysis
Reviewing conversations against a checklist — objection handling, next step, tone — without listening to everything manually.
### Assistants for the team
Helpers for internal work: searching documents and policies, drafting replies and proposals.
### Automated reports
Scheduled summaries for management from the CRM and ad accounts, in plain language.
### Content systems
Generating visuals, video and avatars for content and ads, with quality control.
### Data safety
Which data may be sent to AI services, where it is stored and who has access.

## FAQ
Will an AI agent replace our managers? |
No, and we do not aim for that. The agent takes the routine — standard answers, clarifications, reminders — while negotiations, complex cases and decisions stay with people.
What if the agent gives a wrong answer? |
We restrict it to your data and instructions, forbid answers outside its scope and define hand-over to a person. In the first weeks answers are reviewed manually.
Is it safe to send our data to AI? |
It depends on the service and its settings. Before launch we decide which data can and cannot be used and choose a solution that meets your data-storage requirements.
How much does AI implementation cost? |
It combines usage-based service fees with our setup. We start with one task and a prototype to prove the effect before larger spend, and quote after the free initial review.
Does the agent work in WhatsApp? |
Yes, it can be connected to WhatsApp, Telegram, a website widget and other channels through official integrations.
