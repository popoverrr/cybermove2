# Business website development
We build websites that bring in leads and are connected to sales: from structure and copy to launch, analytics and support. A landing page for ads, a corporate website or an online store — built for the task, not a template.
SITE · CRM · VOIP · AI · AUTO
A good-looking website that the sales team never sees is a common story: leads land in an inbox, calls are not tracked, and ads point to a page that does not answer the customer's main question. So we start with meaning and the customer journey, and we finish not at publication but at verification: every lead reaches the CRM, and every traffic source shows its cost per customer. For companies in Kazakhstan we account for local habits: WhatsApp as the main channel, bilingual versions, maps and local search.

## What's included
### Structure and prototype
Which pages you need, what goes on each, and in what order a visitor gets answers and makes a decision.
### Copywriting
Short and specific: what you do, for whom, why you can be trusted and what to do next. No filler about a team of professionals.
### Design
A visual language that fits the brand and audience, designed mobile-first because the phone is the first screen for most customers.
### Development
Fast pages, forms, messenger buttons, catalogues and landing pages for services and campaigns.
### Analytics and goals
Goals for forms, calls and messenger clicks plus UTM tagging, so you can see which channel brings customers.
### CRM integration
Website leads go straight into the CRM with their source instead of getting lost in a manager's inbox.
### SEO foundations
Structure, headings, speed and markup that let the site be promoted in search without a rebuild.
### Support
Edits, new pages and ongoing checks that forms and analytics keep working after launch.

## FAQ
How much does website development cost? |
It depends on type and scope: a landing page, a multi-section corporate website or a catalogue with integrations are priced differently. We quote after the free initial review.
How long does it take? |
A landing page usually takes a few weeks, a corporate website one to three months. Approving copy takes longest, which is why we write it before design.
Who writes the copy? |
We do, based on the brief and interviews with you. Your part is to check facts and product wording; we handle the rest.
Can you improve our existing site instead? |
Yes. After a review we say plainly which is better value: improving the current site or building a new one. Sometimes new landing pages and proper analytics are enough.
Will the site be bilingual? |
If needed, yes: Russian and English versions, and Kazakh if required. Each version gets proper URLs and markup so search engines do not mix them up.
