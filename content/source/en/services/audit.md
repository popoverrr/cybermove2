# Business audit | Diagnosis first. Budget second. |
Every engagement starts with an audit: we find where the business loses money and clients, and what will drive growth fastest.
CAC · LTV · ROMI · CASH FLOW · MARGIN · CR
An audit is where every CYBERMOVE engagement starts. We look at the whole business: product and marketing, sales, finances and the cost of growth. We work from CRM exports, ad accounts and bookkeeping data rather than impressions. The outcome is a map of problems and opportunities, priorities and a plan you can carry out in-house or with us. Each of the four services can be ordered on its own.
“Financial audit” here means management-level financial diagnostics of a business, not a statutory audit of financial statements. “Investment” means investing in your own business, not securities advice.
