# Corporate legal support
We maintain corporate documentation and legal coordination for your business: shareholder resolutions, internal policies, the legal side of tenders and work with external lawyers. Order in the paperwork and predictability in disputes.
TENDER · BID · CONTRACT · CORP
Corporate legal support is not only about registering a company; it is ongoing order: up-to-date charter documents, formal resolutions, clear shareholder rights and internal rules. While things go well this is easy to neglect; problems surface when a partner joins, a director changes, an inspection arrives or a tender demands documents. A retainer keeps this in order without an in-house lawyer, while complex and specialised issues are handled together with dedicated specialists.
01
## What's included
### Corporate documents
Charter, shareholder resolutions and minutes, amendments to an LLP's constituent documents.
### Company changes
Changes of director, participants, shares or address — documents and the step-by-step procedure.
### Internal documents
Policies, regulations and job descriptions required by law and useful for running the company.
### Legal coordination of projects
The legal side of new business lines, partnerships and joint projects: who is responsible for what and how results are shared.
### Tender paperwork
Legal documents and certificates required to take part in procurement.
### Working with external lawyers
Briefing and overseeing specialists in tax, litigation and intellectual property.

## FAQ
What does a legal retainer include? |
Day-to-day documents, advice and support for changes within an agreed monthly scope. We set the scope based on the actual workload after reviewing your documents.
How much does corporate legal support cost? |
It depends on the volume of work and the format — one-off tasks or a retainer. We quote after the free initial review.
Do you help with LLP registration and changes? |
Yes, we prepare documents for changes — director, participants, shares, address — and describe the procedure. The registration itself goes through government services.
Who handles tax and litigation matters? |
Specialist lawyers and advocates whom we bring in and coordinate. You get one person responsible for the overall legal picture and specialists for specific tasks.
Do you work with companies outside Kazakhstan? |
Corporate support is provided under Kazakh law. For other jurisdictions we coordinate with local lawyers.
Where do we start if the documents have not been updated for years? |
With a review: we collect all constituent and internal documents, compare them with how the company actually works and list what to fix first by level of risk.
