# Tender monitoring
We find tenders that fit your company and assess your chances and margin before you bid. For suppliers and contractors who want to take part in tenders systematically rather than by chance.
TENDER · BID · CONTRACT · CORP
Finding a tender is easy; picking the ones worth bidding on is the hard part. Experience and document requirements, delivery deadlines, payment and security terms, competitors' prices in past tenders — all of this should be checked before your team spends time on a bid. We set up monitoring of public procurement on the goszakup.gov.kz portal and of large companies' purchases on their own platforms, shortlist suitable lots and give a short assessment of each: bid or not, and why.
01
## What's included
### Monitoring setup
Keywords, product and service codes, regions, amounts and procurement types to track — tested against past tenders.
### Platforms
The public procurement portal plus the procurement platforms of large and state-owned companies when your profile fits them.
### Shortlisting
Daily selection of relevant lots without the noise of hundreds of irrelevant notices.
### Requirement analysis
Qualification requirements, documents, deadlines, payment and security terms — anything that could get a bid rejected.
### Competitor analysis
Who took part in similar tenders, at what prices, and who won.
### Profitability check
Whether margin survives price cuts during bidding, logistics, bid security and payment terms.

## FAQ
Which platforms do you monitor? |
Primarily the goszakup.gov.kz public procurement portal. If your profile suits large and state-owned companies, we add their procurement platforms, which have their own rules and registration.
How much does tender monitoring cost? |
It depends on the number of product lines, regions and platforms, and whether detailed tender assessments are needed. We quote after the free initial review.
What does a company need to take part in public procurement in Kazakhstan? |
Registration on the portal with an electronic digital signature and the documents a specific tender requires. During profiling we check what is missing so you do not find out on the last day before the deadline.
Can you also prepare the bid? |
Yes, monitoring is usually combined with bid preparation: shortlisted tenders go straight into work under the same process.
Do you guarantee a win? |
No, nobody can guarantee a win: price, requirements and competitors decide it. We raise your chances by choosing suitable tenders and preparing correct bids.
