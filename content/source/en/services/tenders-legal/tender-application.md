# Tender bid preparation
We prepare the documents, submit the bid and support the procurement until the contract is signed. For companies that want to bid without rejections over formal mistakes.
TENDER · BID · CONTRACT · CORP
Bids are often rejected not on price but on paperwork: the wrong format, an expired certificate, a mismatch with the technical specification, an unsigned file. Full-service tender support removes that risk: we analyse the tender requirements, assemble the document package, check it against the conditions, submit on time and then run the process — answering questions, tracking protocols and helping sign the contract. The document package stays with you and speeds up the next bid.
01
## What's included
### Tender documentation review
Participant requirements, technical specification, draft contract, deadlines and submission rules.
### Document package
The list of documents for the tender, checks on certificate validity and form compliance.
### Technical part
Compliance of the offered goods or services with the specification, descriptions and supporting documents.
### Price offer
Pricing that accounts for costs, logistics, bid security and the minimum acceptable margin.
### Submission
Uploading documents and signing with the participant's electronic digital signature on time, with a buffer for technical glitches.
### Support during bidding
Answering the customer's questions, tracking protocols and results, and challenging violations together with a lawyer.
### Contract signing
Reviewing the draft contract, performance security and signing deadlines.

## FAQ
How much does tender bid preparation cost? |
It depends on the complexity of the tender and the volume of documents; one-off bids and ongoing support are priced differently. We quote after the free initial review.
How early should we contact you? |
The earlier the better: some certificates take several days to obtain. Ideally right after the tender is published, leaving time to ask the customer questions about the documentation.
Why are bids most often rejected? |
Mismatches with qualification requirements or the technical specification, incomplete packages, expired certificates and form violations. Most of these can be spotted early by reading the documentation carefully.
Do we need our own electronic signature? |
Yes, the bid is signed by the participant with its own electronic digital signature. We prepare everything for signing and check that the signature is valid.
Do you work with corporate procurement too? |
Yes, including tenders run by large and state-owned companies. They have their own rules and platforms, so we analyse the requirements for each specific tender.
What if we lose the tender? |
We review the protocol — who won, at what price, whether our bid had remarks — and apply the lessons to the next bids.
