# TikTok ads management
We run TikTok ads built for the platform: videos that look like content, Spark Ads, traffic and lead campaigns, and creator partnerships. For businesses looking for a new customer channel.
CPL · CAC · ROMI · CTR
TikTok advertising works differently from other social networks: the first seconds decide everything, and banner aesthetics lose to authentic content. That makes creative strategy more important than fine-tuning audiences. We start with the videos your audience will actually watch, produce series of variations, test them quickly and scale those that bring inquiries. We work with creators where their voice is more convincing than the brand's.

## What's included
### Creative strategy
Topics, formats and scripts that feel native in the feed and lead to action.
### Video production
Shooting and editing vertical video, variation series for testing, AI creatives where appropriate.
### Traffic and lead campaigns
Ads that send people to your website or use an in-app lead form — chosen by lead quality.
### Spark Ads
Boosting organic posts from the brand or creators while keeping likes and comments.
### Creator partnerships
Choosing creators for your audience, briefs, integrations and using their videos in ads.
### Analytics
Pixel, events, cost per lead and per customer, watch-through and retention, so you know why a video works or not.

## FAQ
Does TikTok work for B2B? |
Less often than for consumer products, but it can work for hiring, an executive's personal brand and awareness. We check fit before launch so the budget is not wasted.
How much do TikTok ads cost? |
It combines the ad budget with video production and campaign management. We quote our fee after the free initial review.
Do we need a brand account on TikTok? |
Not for ads, but an active account builds trust and provides material for Spark Ads. We often combine ads with organic content.
How many videos do we need for a test? |
Several different variations at the start — one video is not enough to tell what works. The exact number is set in the creative strategy.
What are Spark Ads? |
A format where ads run on an already published video from the brand or a creator. Reactions and comments stay, and the ad looks like regular content.
Can TikTok leads go straight into our CRM? |
Yes. Leads from lead forms and the website are passed to the CRM automatically with their source, so TikTok can be compared with other channels by cost per customer.
