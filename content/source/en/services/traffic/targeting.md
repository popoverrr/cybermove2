# Meta ads management
We set up and run ads on Instagram, Facebook and other social networks: audiences, creatives, testing and scaling. We measure cost per lead and per customer, not clicks and reach.
CPL · CAC · ROMI · CTR
Setting up targeting is the easy part. The hard part is understanding why one combination brings leads at a sensible price while another burns budget — and seeing it in time. So before launch we agree on the goal and what counts as a lead, set up event tracking from the website and CRM, then test creatives and audiences in batches and scale what pays off. Reports are built on cost per lead and per customer rather than impressive reach.
01
## What's included
### Strategy and campaign structure
Goals, funnel and budgets by stage: awareness, interest, lead, repeat purchase.
### Audiences
Interests, lookalikes, website and social retargeting, and exclusions so you do not pay to reach existing customers.
### Creatives and testing
Series of image and video variations for feed, Reels and Stories, tested against a plan.
### Lead forms or landing pages
Native lead forms or a landing page — chosen by lead quality, not just cost.
### Event tracking
Pixel and Conversions API so algorithms learn from leads and sales, not clicks.
### Optimisation
Switching off weak combinations, reallocating budget and managing frequency.
### CPL and CAC reports
Cost per lead and per customer by campaign, creative and audience, with conclusions and a test plan.

## FAQ
How much does Meta ads management cost? |
It has two parts: the ad budget you pay the platform, and our setup and management fee. The budget depends on your niche and goals; we quote our fee after the free initial review.
What budget do we need to start? |
Enough to gather statistics on several combinations in the first weeks. We calculate it from the cost per lead in your niche and the number of hypotheses to test, rather than quoting a market average.
When will the first leads come in? |
Usually within the first days. A stable cost per lead forms over several weeks of testing, once it is clear which creatives and audiences work.
Who makes the creatives? |
We do: design and editing are included, and we organise shoots when needed. Your part is approval and access to the product.
Why are leads cheap but sales low? |
Often the platform optimises for any lead, including random ones. We send qualified lead and sales data back to the ad account and check the sales process as well.
