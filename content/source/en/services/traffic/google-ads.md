# Google Ads management services
We set up and manage Google advertising: Search, Display, Performance Max, YouTube and remarketing. We bring in people already searching for your product and measure what a customer costs.
CPL · CAC · ROMI · CTR
Google Ads works with high-intent demand: a person is searching for the service, and you need to appear with a clear offer. Ad position depends not only on the bid but also on quality — how well the ad and landing page match the search. So our setup starts with structure and keywords, continues with landing pages and conversion tracking, and only then moves to bidding. Without correct conversions, automated strategies optimise for the wrong thing.

## What's included
### Keywords and structure
The searches people use to find your product, grouped by intent, with negative keywords so you do not pay for irrelevant traffic.
### Ads and assets
Copy for every ad group, plus call, location, sitelink and callout assets.
### Conversion tracking
Leads, calls and purchases sent to Google Ads, and CRM sales data where possible.

Search campaigns for high-intent demand, automated campaigns where there is enough data to learn from.
### Display and YouTube
Display and video ads for reach and for reminding past website visitors.
### Remarketing
Bringing back visitors who did not convert, with a dedicated offer.
### Bid and budget optimisation
Bidding strategies, budget reallocation and switching off wasteful searches and placements.

## FAQ
How much does Google Ads management cost? |
It has two parts: the ad budget you pay Google, and our setup and management fee. We quote our fee after the free initial review.
Why do clicks keep getting more expensive? |
Competitors raise bids in the auction, and cost per click also depends on ad and landing page quality. Improving relevance lets you pay less for the same position; we cover this in the audit.
Search or Performance Max — which is better? |
For services with clear demand we start with Search: it is more transparent and controllable. Performance Max comes in once there are enough conversions for automation to learn from.
Can we advertise without a website? |
You can send traffic to a maps listing or a form, but a website or landing page usually performs better and is easier to measure. If you have none, we can build a landing page for the campaign.
How soon will we see results? |
Impressions and first leads start at launch. A stable cost per lead takes several weeks, once statistics accumulate and irrelevant searches are cleaned out.
