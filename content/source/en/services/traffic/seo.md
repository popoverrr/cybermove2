# SEO services
We grow company websites in Google so customers find you by the services you offer, not just by your name. Technical fixes, service pages, content, links and local search — without promises of the top spot in a month.
CPL · CAC · ROMI · CTR
SEO for a service company is first of all the right structure: a dedicated page for each service that answers the customer's questions, a technically sound site and content that shows expertise. Then links and local signals do their work: a business profile on maps, reviews and mentions. Search optimisation does not work overnight — rankings for services usually appear within months — but traffic grows without paying for every click.

## FAQ
When will SEO show results? |
Technical fixes show within weeks; rankings for services usually take several months depending on competition and the site's starting point. Nobody, including us, can guarantee search positions.
How much do SEO services cost? |
It depends on site size, competition in your niche and content volume. We quote after the audit and the free initial review.
Do we need articles on the website? |
For a service business, yes: articles answer customers' questions before they contact you and attract informational searches. But we start with service pages, which bring leads.
Do you buy links? |
We do not use schemes that search engines penalise. Links and mentions come from industry, partner and local sites where they belong.
What is local SEO? |
Ranking in search and on maps for queries with a city name or "near me". It needs a Google Business Profile, reviews and the same name, address and phone on the website and in directories.
Can a bilingual website be optimised? |
Yes, and in Kazakhstan it often makes sense. Each language version needs its own URLs, hreflang markup and copy written for searches in that language rather than machine translation.
