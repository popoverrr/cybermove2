# PR services for companies
Public relations for companies and their leaders: PR strategy, media relations, news hooks, reputation and events. The goal is for you to be talked about where your clients and partners make decisions.
CORE · LENS · 9:16 · WAVE · BEAM
Media coverage on its own achieves little if it is unclear what it is for: trust before a deal, recognition in a new market, protecting a reputation. So PR starts with the goal and the audience, and only then do we choose outlets, formats and news hooks. We work with companies and individuals — from promoting political candidates within a large communications team to PR for a law firm. We measure results not by clippings but by how the right people's attitude changes: branded searches, inquiries and the quality of partner conversations.

### PR strategy
Goals, audiences, key messages, outlets and a news calendar for the period.
### Media relations

Mention monitoring, handling reviews and criticism, prepared answers and a crisis plan.

Positioning the leader as an industry expert: talks, columns and interviews.

## FAQ
Do you guarantee publications? |
Nobody can honestly guarantee editorial coverage — the editor decides. We guarantee strong stories and materials and active work with outlets; paid placements, if needed, are labelled separately.
How much do PR services cost? |
It depends on the tasks, the number of directions and whether events are included. We quote after the free initial review.
How is PR different from advertising? |
Advertising is a paid message you control. PR is what others say about you: media, experts, partners. The second is trusted more but is harder to control.
Do you handle crises? |
Yes: we prepare the position and messages and help with media and social media responses. It is better when a crisis plan is prepared in advance rather than during the crisis.
Which media do you work with? |
We choose outlets for the project's audience: business and trade media, city publications, specialist blogs and podcasts.
How do you measure PR results? |
By outlet quality and reach, mentions of key messages, growth in branded searches and inquiries after publications. We agree on these metrics in advance so evaluation does not become counting clippings.
