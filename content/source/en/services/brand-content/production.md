# Content production for brands
We produce photos, videos, reels and ad creatives from idea to finished files for every platform. Content that goes out on social media and performs in ads instead of sitting in a folder after the shoot.
CORE · LENS · 9:16 · WAVE · BEAM
Shooting social content and producing ads are different jobs, but they fail the same way: the footage looks great, then it turns out the formats are wrong and the videos lack what actually sells. We start with the goal and the platform: what the viewer should do, where they will see it and how many seconds we have. We write scripts for that, plan the shoot day to capture as much material as possible and edit a whole series of videos and creatives at once.

## What's included
### Concept and script
An idea built for the goal — awareness, leads, product launch — and a storyboard for each format.
### Shoot organisation
Crew, location, talent, props and timing; a production day that yields content for weeks.

Product, brand and reportage shoots, vertical videos for Reels and TikTok.
### Editing and graphics
Editing, colour grading, captions and motion graphics, versions for sound on and sound off.
### Ad creatives
Series of variations for testing in social and Google ads, cut for each placement.
### AI video and avatars
Generated visuals, video and digital avatars where they are faster and appropriate.
### Large projects
Ad campaigns, events and broadcasts with contractor coordination and logistics.

## FAQ
How much does video production cost? |
It depends on the number of shoot days, crew, locations and editing volume. After the brief we provide an itemised estimate; the initial review is free.
How many videos come out of one shoot day? |
It depends on the format: with good preparation, one production day yields a series of short videos and photos for several weeks of posting. The exact number is fixed in the shot plan before the shoot.
Who owns the rights to the materials? |
Usage terms are agreed in the contract before the shoot: where, for how long and in which formats the materials can be used, including talent and music rights.
Can you shoot in Almaty and other cities? |
Yes, we organise shoots where the project needs them; logistics and a local crew are included in the estimate.
When is AI video appropriate? |
For quick creative tests, visuals and content where the real product is not on screen. For trust in the brand, people and product we still recommend live shoots.
Can one shoot cover several platforms? |
Yes, that is how we plan it: one shoot day gives vertical videos for Reels and TikTok, horizontal versions for YouTube and the website, photos and ad creatives. The format list is approved before the shoot.
