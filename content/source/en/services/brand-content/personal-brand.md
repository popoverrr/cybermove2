# Personal branding services
We build personal brands that sell: positioning, a content system, product and course launches, and PR for the person. Our experience includes six years with Oleksandr Usyk's personal brand, promoting radio hosts and launching experts.
CORE · LENS · 9:16 · WAVE · BEAM
A personal brand is not a follower count but how much a person's name helps sell: consultations, courses, company products, partnerships. We start with positioning — where the person is stronger than others and for whom — then build a content system that can be sustained without burning out, and turn audience attention into launches and sales.

## FAQ
Do I have to run my own social media? |
Your face and voice stay yours; the rest can be delegated: scripts, shooting, editing, posting and replies. We design the system so it needs a few hours a week from you.
How much do personal branding services cost? |
It depends on the scope: positioning and a content system only, or product launches and PR as well. We quote after the free initial review.
How soon will I see results? |
Positioning and the content system take the first weeks. Growing recognition and sales through a personal brand takes months of consistent work; promising quick results without a product launch would be dishonest.
Do you help launch a course? |
Yes: from the idea and packaging to the technical side — platform, chatbots, payments — and launch support. We have done this with several authors and experts.
Do you work with people outside the media? |
Yes. Most personal brands belong to founders and experts, not media personalities. For them we build the brand around expertise and results.
What if I already have an audience but no income from it? |
That is common: the audience exists, but there is no product or clear offer. We start with a diagnosis — who the followers are and what they expect — and build a product and launch around that audience.
