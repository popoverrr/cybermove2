# Social media management services
We run social media so it builds trust and sales: presence strategy, content plan, production and posting, community management and metric reports. For businesses that need results, not just scheduled posts.
CORE · LENS · 9:16 · WAVE · BEAM
Social media for business often becomes posting for its own sake: neat, regular and with no effect on sales. We start by asking what role social media plays in the customer journey — first acquaintance, choosing between you and a competitor, or coming back for another purchase. That decides the content pillars, formats, frequency and how we measure results. Instagram and TikTok growth is built on short vertical videos and series that can be boosted with ads.

### Presence strategy

Posting to plan, replying to comments and messages, moderation.

Promotions, interactive formats, collaborations with creators and local partners.

Reach, engagement, clicks and inquiries — and what we change in the plan each month.

## FAQ
How much does social media management cost? |
It depends on the number of platforms, the volume of content and whether shoots are needed. We quote after the free initial review.
When will we see results? |
Changes in reach and engagement show in the first weeks; the effect on inquiries usually takes one to two months of consistent work, depending on where the account starts.
Do we need ads as well? |
Ideally, yes. Organic reach is limited, and promoting the best posts helps them reach the right people. The ad budget is discussed separately.
Who creates the content? |
We can shoot it on production days or work with your materials. Often we combine a monthly shoot day with quick content from your team.
Which networks do you manage? |
Mainly Instagram and TikTok, plus Facebook, YouTube and Telegram when needed. We choose platforms by audience, not by trend.
Can we run social media without the owner on camera? |
Yes. Employees, customers, the product and the work process can all be the face of the brand. The owner's presence builds trust but is not required for every company.
