# Brand positioning services
We put into words how you differ and why customers should choose you — so that clients, your team and contractors understand it the same way. The brand core then becomes the base for your website, content, advertising and sales.
CORE · LENS · 9:16 · WAVE · BEAM
Without positioning, every contractor reinvents the brand: the designer in the logo, the copywriter in the texts, the ads specialist in the creatives. The result is a set of disconnected messages that are hard to believe. Positioning work starts from what already exists — the product, customers, competitors, real advantages. We assemble this into an eight-element brand core and test whether it holds up against the market.
01
## What's included
### Product and audience
What you really sell and who buys it: segments, their jobs to be done, fears and reasons to choose.
### Positioning
Where the brand stands against competitors: what makes you first, best or only for your customer.
### Value and difference
What the customer gets and why they cannot get it the same way elsewhere; proof rather than promises.
### Offer and message
The offer and core messages for the website, ads and sales, phrased briefly and verifiably.
### Brand DNA and tone of voice
Character, values and way of speaking that make every text and video recognisable.
### Brand strategy and architecture
How products and sub-brands relate, what grows under one name and what stands alone.
### Naming
Names for the company, products or lines when needed, checked for meaning and memorability.

## FAQ
How is positioning different from a logo and visual identity? |
Positioning is the meaning: for whom, what and why. Logo and identity are its visual expression. Without positioning, design becomes a matter of taste, so we start with the core.
How much does brand positioning cost? |
It depends on the number of products and segments and the depth of research. We quote after the free initial review.
How long does it take? |
Usually three to four weeks from research to brand platform. Customer interviews take the most time and give the most precise wording.
Does the owner need to be involved? |
Yes, in the research and the strategy session. Positioning the owner does not share will not survive its first conflict with sales.
What do we do with the brand platform afterwards? |
Apply it: rewrite the website, update creatives, train the sales team. We can do this ourselves or help your contractors.
Can positioning be done without customer research? |
It can, but the result will be weaker: the wording will sound like the company rather than the customer. If interviews are impossible, we use reviews, chats and call recordings, which also show why people buy from you.
