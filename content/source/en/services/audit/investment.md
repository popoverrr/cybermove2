# Cost reduction consulting
We review your costs and investment plans: what can be cut safely, what returns money fastest and what budget growth really needs. For owners facing a decision about money.
CAC · LTV · ROMI · CASH FLOW · MARGIN · CR
This is about investing in your own business — marketing, sales, systems, people, new locations — not advice on securities. Cutting costs blindly is risky: sometimes the most expensive line brings in most of the revenue, and a cheap contractor ends up costing the most through lost leads. So every decision is checked against unit economics, with a payback estimate and a view of what happens if it does not work.
“Financial audit” here means management-level financial diagnostics of a business, not a statutory audit of financial statements. “Investment” means investing in your own business, not securities advice.
01
## What's included
### Cost analysis
Fixed and variable costs, how they relate to revenue, lines that grow faster than the business and costs without a clear owner.
### Marketing and sales budget
How much to spend on acquisition and retention so customer cost stays within margin, and how to split it across channels.
### Investment priorities
Which initiatives pay back fastest — lead tracking, advertising, product, team, a new location — and what can wait.
### Growth scenarios
Cautious, base and bold scenarios with payback periods and break-even points.
### Savings
Contractors, tools, subscriptions and processes that can be replaced, merged or automated without losing quality.
### Impact tracking
Metrics that show within a month or two whether the change worked or should be revisited.

## FAQ
Do you give investment advice? |
No. We talk about investing in your own business — marketing, systems, people, locations. We do not advise on securities, deposits or other financial instruments.
How much can we save? |
We do not promise a figure upfront; it depends on your cost structure. After the review we name specific lines, expected savings for each and the risks of cutting them.
How much does the work cost? |
It depends on the size of the business and the number of lines. We quote after the free initial review.
Will quality suffer after the cuts? |
That is why we cut by contribution to revenue, not by size of the line. For every decision we describe what may get worse and how to track it.
Can this be combined with a financial health check? |
Yes, that is common: the financial review shows the picture first, and the cost and investment plan is built on it.
