# Sales and marketing audit
For owners who pay for advertising and a sales team but are not sure the money works. We review the product, marketing and sales process and show where customers are lost and what to fix first.
CAC · LTV · ROMI · CASH FLOW · MARGIN · CR
A marketing audit without a sales audit tells half the story: leads can be cheap and still never close. So we look at the whole path — from the offer and the ads to payment and repeat purchase — and work from CRM exports, ad accounts and analytics rather than the team's impressions. The outcome is not a list of general advice but numbers for your funnel and a ranked plan.
01
## What's included
### Product and offer
What you actually sell, to whom, and why people buy from you today; where the offer loses to competitors on price, promise or terms.
### Positioning and competitors
How the market sees you and how you really differ. We review key competitors: prices, promises, channels, strengths and weaknesses.
### Channels and funnel
Where leads come from, what each one costs, and which step of the funnel loses the most money.
### Customer journey
From first contact to payment: response time, follow-ups, reasons for lost deals and repeat purchases.
### Sales team
Scripts, call and chat reviews, objection handling and CRM discipline — a proper sales team audit.
### Analytics and data
What is measured and what is not: goals, lead sources and reports a manager can actually base decisions on.
### Website and touchpoints
Pages, forms, messengers and phone lines — where a lead can get lost for purely technical reasons.

Long sales cycles, several decision-makers, tenders: manufacturing, engineering, industrial supply.

## FAQ
How much does a business audit cost? |
It depends on scale: the number of products and channels, the amount of data and the number of locations. We quote after the initial review, which is free and takes one meeting.
How long does it take? |
Usually two to four weeks. Data collection takes the most time, so it goes faster where the CRM and ad accounts are already kept in order.
What do you need from us? |
Read-only access to ad accounts, CRM and analytics, sales exports, a contact person in sales, and two or three meetings with the team.
How is an audit different from a consultation? |
A consultation is an opinion on a question; an audit is a check against your own data. You get numbers for your funnel and priorities that turn into a work plan, not general advice.
What happens after the audit? |
You receive the report and the plan. You can implement it yourself, with us on specific areas — website and CRM, advertising, sales — or end to end.
