# Financial health check for business
We show which products, channels and locations make money and which ones eat it. For owners who need a management view, not statements for the tax office.
CAC · LTV · ROMI · CASH FLOW · MARGIN · CR
This is a management-level financial review, not a statutory audit of financial statements. We pull numbers from accounting, bank statements, CRM and ad accounts into one model and answer the questions that decide the budget: what a customer costs to acquire, how much they bring over their lifetime, where the margin goes and when a cash gap may appear. The report is written for an owner, with conclusions you can turn into decisions.
“Financial audit” here means management-level financial diagnostics of a business, not a statutory audit of financial statements. “Investment” means investing in your own business, not securities advice.
01
## What's included
### Unit economics
Customer acquisition cost, lifetime revenue per customer and margin per unit or order.
### Management P&L
Revenue and costs by business line and location in one report you can read without an accountant.
### Cash flow
When money comes in, when it goes out, and a forecast of possible cash gaps and the reserve you need.
### Product and channel profitability
Which products and customer sources pay off and which are subsidised by others.
### Cost structure
Fixed and variable costs, what grows faster than revenue, and what can be cut without losing quality.
### A working model
A spreadsheet model your team can update and use to test decisions before making them.

## FAQ
Is this an audit of financial statements? |
No. It is a management financial review for the owner. Statutory audits are carried out by licensed audit firms; we do not replace that work or issue audit opinions.
How much does it cost? |
It depends on the number of business lines and locations, the state of your bookkeeping and the volume of data. We quote after the free initial review.
How long does it take? |
Usually two to three weeks. Collecting data takes longest; if everything sits in one system and exports are available, it goes faster.
What if our bookkeeping is a mess? |
That is common. We use the sources that exist, cover gaps with clearly labelled assumptions, and add recommendations on which records to set up so the numbers calculate themselves next time.
Who will see our numbers? |
Only the project team. Read-only access is enough, and exports can be shared as files without giving access to your systems.
