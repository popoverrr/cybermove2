# Business growth strategy consulting
We turn the owner's goals into a 3–12 month plan: what to do, in what order and how to tell it is working. For companies that want to grow by plan rather than from task to task.
CAC · LTV · ROMI · CASH FLOW · MARGIN · CR
A growth strategy should not be a deck that ends up in a folder. We connect goals to specific initiatives, resources and metrics so the team knows what to do this week and the owner can check progress every month. The strategy rests on audit data: without knowing where the business loses money today, a growth plan is a set of guesses. If there has been no audit, we run a short diagnosis inside the project.
01
## What's included
### Goals and constraints
Where the company is and where it wants to be, what resources, people and time are really available, and which limits cannot be bypassed.
### Growth strategy
What growth comes from — new customers, repeat sales, average order, new markets or products — and the cost of each route.
### Prioritised initiatives
Opportunities ranked by impact, cost and dependencies, so the first steps unlock the rest.
### Quarterly roadmap
A business development plan by period, with owners and checkpoints.
### KPIs and checkpoints
A few metrics that show whether the plan is on track and what to do if it is not.
### Sales processes
How to organise sales for the new strategy: roles, funnel and rules.

## FAQ
What horizon does the strategy cover? |
Usually 3–12 months broken into quarters. Longer horizons are described as a direction, while the detailed plan stays short enough to remain practical.
How much does it cost? |
It depends on company size, the number of business lines and whether an audit has been done. We quote after the free initial review.
Do we need an audit first? |
Ideally, yes: strategy without diagnosis rests on assumptions. If there has been no audit, we start with a short review of the market, product and funnel within the project.
How is a roadmap different from a strategy? |
Strategy answers what growth will come from; the roadmap answers what we do and when. We deliver both and tie them to KPIs.
Who will execute the plan? |
Whatever suits you: your team, us on selected areas, or together. The plan is written so it can be executed without us.
What if the plan stops working? |
That is what the checkpoints are for. At monthly reviews we compare metrics and adjust priorities without waiting for the end of the quarter.
